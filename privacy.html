CamLink Desktop Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

CamLink Desktop is a Windows viewer and controller for the CamLink Android camera-sharing app. This policy explains what information the desktop app handles and how it is used.

Information handled by the app

  • Camera images and video frames: When you connect to a CamLink phone stream, the app receives and displays camera frames from that phone. Those images may contain people or other information that can identify someone.
  • Connection address: The IP address that you enter is used to connect to the phone on your local network.
  • App preferences: The app saves preferences such as the last camera IP, zoom, exposure, white balance, JPEG quality, resolution, preview mode, sidebar visibility, and window size in a local file named .camlink_desktop.json in your Windows home folder.
  • Snapshots: When you choose Save snapshot, the image is written to the file location you select.

How information is used and shared

The app uses camera frames only to display the live preview and to create a snapshot when you request one. Connection settings are used to restore your preferences next time you launch the app.

The desktop app does not send camera frames, settings, or snapshots to a CamLink cloud service because no such service is used by the app. The app does not require an account and does not intentionally collect analytics or telemetry. The live camera stream travels directly between the phone and computer over your local network.

Network security notice

The current version uses HTTP for the local camera stream and does not provide stream encryption or pairing authentication. Anyone with suitable access to the same network who can reach the phone's camera server may be able to view the stream. Use CamLink only on a trusted network, stop sharing when finished, and do not expose the camera server to the public internet through port forwarding. Do not use the app for sensitive recordings over an untrusted network.

Storage and control

The app's preferences remain in your Windows home folder until you delete the settings file. Snapshots remain wherever you save them, and you can delete them using your usual file-management tools. Stop the camera sharing session on the phone to end the live stream.

Children

CamLink Desktop is a general-purpose camera viewer. Users should obtain the permission of people being recorded and follow applicable laws and school or workplace rules.

Changes to this policy

This page may be updated when the app's data handling changes. The effective date at the top indicates the latest revision.

Contact and support

For support or privacy questions, open an issue in the CamLink Desktop repository.
